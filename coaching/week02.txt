COACH: You're actually very close, Jonathan, but it's the other way around! Let me clarify:

- <%= %> ESCAPES the output — meaning it converts special characters like < and > into safe HTML, which protects against security attacks.
- <%- %> does NOT escape the output — meaning it renders the content as raw HTML.

So looking at your template, <%- include('partials/header') %> uses the unescaped tag because you WANT the header to be rendered as actual HTML, not as plain text.

You had the right idea, just the two got swapped! That's a very easy thing to mix up.
